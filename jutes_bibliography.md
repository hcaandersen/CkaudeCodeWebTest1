## Methodological Notes

All web sources were accessed in October 2025. This bibliography includes both direct sources cited in the timeline and supplementary sources that informed the overall narrative structure and chronological framework.

Cross-referencing was performed across multiple sources to verify dates and events. Where sources conflicted, the timeline notes the uncertainty or provides the range of scholarly opinions.

Wikipedia articles, while included, were cross-referenced with academic sources, museum databases, and specialized historical websites to ensure accuracy. Primary sources (Tacitus, Bede) were accessed through modern translations and scholarly editions available online.

The chronology follows standard archaeological periodization for Scandinavia:
- Bronze Age: c. 1500-500 BC
- Pre-Roman Iron Age: c. 500 BC - 1 AD
- Roman Iron Age: c. 1-400 AD
- Migration Period: c. 400-700 AD

All dates marked "c." (circa) reflect the inherent uncertainties in ancient chronology and archaeological dating methods.
