[32] **Wikipedia**
"Lejre"
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lejre
Description: Comprehensive article on Lejre as the royal seat and capital of the Danes near Zealand, home of the Skjöldung (Scylding) dynasty, counterpart to Heorot in Beowulf, with archaeological settlement complex spanning 550-1000 AD and discovery of Denmark's largest Viking Age house in 2009.
